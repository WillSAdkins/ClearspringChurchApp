Settings you need

SMTP_HOSTYour provider's server, e.g. smtp.gmail.com
SMTP_PORTUsually 587. Defaults to 587 if unset.
SMTP_USERYour login, usually the full email address
SMTP_PASSWORDAn app password, not your normal one
SMTP_FROMThe address people see, e.g. hello@yourchurch.org
SMTP_SECURITYstarttls (default), ssl, or none

Gmail and Outlook both need an app password rather than your account password, and Gmail requires two-factor authentication switched on before it will issue one. Dedicated senders like Brevo, Mailgun or Postmark are more reliable for anything going to more than a handful of people, since a normal mailbox will start being treated as spam.
