How it works

Each lesson builds a verse up a little at a time: put the words in order, fill the gaps, then type the first letter of every word from memory.

Learned verses come back for a quick review just before you'd forget them: after a day, then three, a week, a fortnight, a month. The six dots under each verse show how firmly it's fixed.

To learn any verse, tap it in the Bible and choose Learn.

Every verse is in the World English Bible, modern English that's easy to learn and free to use.
