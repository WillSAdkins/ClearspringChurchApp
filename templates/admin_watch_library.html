Watch library

See it in the app ← Admin

Courses and teaching from YouTube channels the church trusts, shown under Watch → Courses. A course is a YouTube playlist, or a list of videos you choose. The app plays them in YouTube's privacy-enhanced player and credits the people who made them; nothing is copied. The starter list is a suggestion: watch a little of each before you leave it in, and hide or delete anything that isn't right for Clearspring.
