Feature tips: the app advertising itself

When there's no church notice to show, the app can point people to a part of it they haven't tried. On the home page it can show a tip straight away on every visit (the first box below). On the other main tabs it never shows on someone's first day, at most one pop-up per visit, and never for something they already use. Each tip shows at most twice, and anyone can tap “Don’t show tips”.

Sponsor pop-ups

For later, if the church decides to promote a local business or partner charity. They're made here like any pop-up, carry a “Sponsored” label with the sponsor's name, and follow the same rules: once per person, never more than one per visit. There's no ad network and nothing tracks anyone; you'll see anonymous totals (seen and tapped) to report back to a sponsor. Before switching this on, check it with the trustees, update the privacy page and the app store listings (which say “no adverts”), and keep sponsors suitable for children.
