Word by word (grammar.sqlite, v32)
----------------------------------
Grammar codes and their expansions: STEPBible.org, Tyndale House, Cambridge
  (TAGNT, TAHOT, TEGMC, TEHMC), CC BY 4.0. https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data
Sentence structure, participant references, Septuagint equivalents:
  MACULA Greek (SBLGNT trees) and MACULA Hebrew (WLC trees), Biblica, Inc. / Clear Bible,
  CC BY 4.0. https://github.com/Clear-Bible/macula-greek  https://github.com/Clear-Bible/macula-hebrew
Meaning-group (semantic domain) labels: United Bible Societies MARBLE, Semantic Dictionary of
  Biblical Greek, based on J. P. Louw and E. A. Nida, Greek-English Lexicon of the New Testament
  Based on Semantic Domains (UBS, 1996); supplied in MACULA Greek and used with permission.
Changes made for Clearspring: words aligned to the STEPBible word lists; grammar explanations
  rewritten in plain English; clause layout derived from the syntax trees; Septuagint links
  aggregated by Strong's number. Rebuild with tools/build_grammar.py.
